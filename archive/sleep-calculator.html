Sleep Calculator

Sleep runs in ~90‑minute cycles. Waking up mid‑cycle is what makes you feel groggy — this works out times that land you between cycles instead.

Result

If you go to sleep at one of the times above, you'll rise between 90‑minute sleep cycles. A good night's sleep is 5–6 complete cycles.

Why 90-minute cycles?

A full sleep cycle — moving through light sleep, deep sleep, and REM — takes roughly 90 minutes. Waking up at the end of a completed cycle generally feels far more refreshing than waking up in the middle of one, even if the total hours slept are similar.

The average time to fall asleep

Most people take about 15 minutes to actually fall asleep after getting into bed. This calculator adds that on top of the cycle math automatically.

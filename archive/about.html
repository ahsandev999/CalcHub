About

Small tools, kept simple.

CalcHub is a small set of free calculators built for quick, everyday questions — no sign-up, no app, no clutter.

What this site is

Each tool on this site does one job well: the sleep calculator works out good bedtimes or wake times, the age calculator gives an exact age from a birth date, and the basic calculator handles quick everyday sums. That's it — no accounts, no tracking dashboards, nothing to install.

Contact

Questions or feedback about these tools can be sent via the contact details on this page once they're added — for now, this is a small independent project maintained by one person.
